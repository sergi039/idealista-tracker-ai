Scoring config (advanced)
Optional per-profile override (JSON)
Expected shape (example):
{"categories":{"housing":{"investment":{"value_score":0.6,"travel_score":0.25,"sea_score":0.15},"lifestyle":{"travel_score":0.45,"size_score":0.3,"sea_score":0.25},"travel_minutes":{"best":10,"worst":60},"sea_distance":{"near_m":300,"far_m":10000}}}}
sea_distance: straight-line metres to the coastline. The shoreline scores 100, near_m sets how fast the score falls away from it, and far_m is where it reaches 0. Keep far_m at or below 15 km: that is how far the coastline lookup actually searches, and a wider horizon leaves inland listings unscored rather than at 0.
To clear: empty the JSON and save.
